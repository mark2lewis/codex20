---
name: Cross-mode visual direction
description: Design constraint for the Codex Dynamics public site and CRM.
---

Use opaque, calm surfaces and clear borders across the public site and CRM in both light and dark modes. Accounting and client-ledger buttons should be compact and visibly styled with the CRM's color treatment. Avoid glass blur and decorative glow.

**Why:** The user said the CRM was still far from the desired restrained Apple-like feel and specifically asked for no glass or glow, while noting that many surfaces and text colors became too bright, faint, or invisible when switching modes. The user said Accounting and ledger buttons were too large and did not match the CRM, then repeated that they still lacked styling and color.

**How to apply:** When adjusting appearance or theme behavior, favor neutral surfaces, legible foregrounds, visible but quiet borders, compact and clearly colored button controls in Accounting and client ledgers, and verify both light and dark modes. Do not add translucent blur or ornamental glow.
